About Us

This system was created in 2026 by the PixelNature team as part of our mission to support the UCI Nature Reserve and its ongoing efforts in tracking and conserving local wildlife. By transforming thousands of raw camera images into meaningful, organized datasets, we hope to empower researchers to better understand and protect our natural ecosystems.

Our Mission: Helping Conservation Grow Better

We believe not just in gathering data, but in making it meaningful. By automating species detection across thousands of camera images, we empower researchers to efficiently turn raw data into actionable insights. This pipeline allows conservationists to focus on what matters most: understanding and protecting our natural world.
